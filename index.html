Artist statement and fabrication spec

Between equation and object

Each sculpture begins as a rule, not a sketch. A gyroid is a triply periodic minimal surface whose mean curvature vanishes at every point. A Menger sponge removes the middle ninth of every division of a cube, keeping only cells whose base-three coordinates contain at most one middle digit. A helix maps angle and height into three rotating ribbons. A Voronoi terrace grows from the distance between competing seed points. Sampling these continuous ideas onto an integer voxel lattice turns mathematical structure into something you can inspect one cell at a time.

From lattice to manufactured object

The lattice is a manifold surface before it becomes a file. Exposed face quads are merged on shared lattice coordinates, then checked for paired directed edges, connected vertex-link cycles, Euler characteristic and divergence-theorem volume. Export a solid as ASCII or binary STL for slicing, OBJ for a modeling workflow, or OpenSCAD to rebuild the exact cuboid occupancy. The project JSON preserves every cell and its pitch for later editions.
